# Pitching the free pilot

Edit the bracketed bits. Send it in person or by WhatsApp to businesses you already use. Warm introductions convert far better than cold messages.

## Short WhatsApp message

> Hi [name], it's [your name], I'm a regular at [business]. I'm building an AI booking assistant for WhatsApp as part of my portfolio, and I'm looking for one local business to pilot it **for free** for a month.
>
> It answers common questions, books appointments into a calendar, can take deposits through Yoco or PayFast, and sends reminders to cut no-shows. Anything it isn't sure about comes straight to you.
>
> All I'd ask is about 30 minutes to set it up with your prices and FAQs, and a short testimonial if it helps. Could I show you a 2-minute demo this week?

## What to say in the meeting (the owner cares about these, not about AI)
- **Fewer missed bookings:** it replies at 10pm and on Sundays, when you can't.
- **Fewer no-shows:** automatic reminders, and deposits if you want them.
- **You stay in control:** complaints, refunds, discounts and anything unusual come to you. It never makes up prices or policies.
- **No cost, no lock-in:** free for the pilot, it uses your existing Yoco or PayFast account, and you can switch it off at any time.
- **Their customers' privacy:** it follows POPIA, customers are told it's an assistant, and data is deleted after an agreed period.

## Before the meeting
- Have the project 1 demo (Slack escalation) or a short recording ready, to show you build reliable things.
- Bring `business-info.yaml` printed, or on your phone, to fill in together.
