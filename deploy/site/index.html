A small automation, operated by Nephtally Moshabeshe, that handles enquiries from a salon website's contact form:

  1. The website sends the enquiry to this server.
  2. An AI model sorts it (booking, price question, complaint, and so on) and drafts a reply using only the salon's published prices and policies.
  3. Automatic checks catch mistakes, such as prices that aren't on the price list or a date that doesn't match the weekday.
  4. The enquiry is logged to a Google Sheet and emailed to the person who replies. A human always sends the actual reply.
